What I do

I believe a growth plan should start with the audience you want, and only then the channel.

Across more than 500 hours of one-on-one coaching with news founders and newsroom leaders, the pattern I've seen time and time again goes something like this: the membership program is built, the donation forms work, the welcome series is written, and the list just isn't growing fast enough to feed any of it. It's a bit like a restaurant with a terrific kitchen and a half-empty dining room (the food is fine, people just don't know you're open).

Here's the thing that I think most plans skip: before you choose a channel, it helps to know which audience is actually worth pursuing, how many of those people are out there, and what it will cost to reach them. That's where my work starts, and from there it usually covers some mix of the following:

  • Market sizing and audience segmentation, so you know how much room you have to grow and who is in it.
  • Paid acquisition strategy and channel selection, including the channels outside Meta that most newsrooms haven't tested yet.
  • Organic social, including vertical video as a way for a small newsroom to get discovered.
  • Partnerships with other publishers and creators, from newsletter swaps to joint projects.
  • In-person events that bring new people onto your list.

Each of these gets run as a small, structured experiment with a number attached, because I'd much rather we learn something real in a few weeks than guess our way through a year.

One newsroom at a time, and only a few at once.

We meet monthly or every other week with you and whoever owns audience on your team. Between sessions there's a short list of experiments to run, and a shared document that tracks what we tested and what it taught us.
