What I do

Most of my week is spent inside newsrooms, working on the numbers that keep them going.

Coaching with me is hands-on and specific to your newsroom. We pick the growth problem that matters most right now, design a small experiment around it, and look at what happened together. The work usually touches some mix of these:

  • Reaching the people in your community who don't know you exist yet, through paid campaigns, partnerships and search.
  • Turning casual visitors into newsletter subscribers, and making sure the welcome series earns their attention.
  • Asking for money well, whether that's membership, donations or a year-end campaign.
  • Getting your analytics configured properly so you can tell which experiments actually worked.

About

I've spent more than two decades on the business side of independent journalism.

I was the CTO of The Tyee, where I worked with the executive team on fundraising that raised more than $1M for its investigative reporting. I was later Mozilla's Senior Fellow for Media & Trust, and a John S. Knight Journalism Fellow at Stanford, where I piloted the entrepreneurship training that became the GNI Pre-launch Lab.

These days I spend most of my time coaching newsroom leaders, and I live and work in Napa, California.
